## Brand & Style
The design system expresses the authentic identity, competitive stamina, and community pride of Club Balonmano Buñol. Designed for players, coaching staff, federation delegates, and supporters across the Valencian handball circuit, the visual environment balances high-impact court energy with institutional clarity.

The visual style is **Corporate Athletic Modernism**. It prioritizes clean, high-contrast, light-first surfaces reflective of bright indoor pavilion floors and official federation records, reinforced with bold competitive color blocking. The emotional response is confident, spirited, and disciplined: energetic match-day intensity backed by clean administrative rigor.

## Colors
The palette is rooted directly in the club's official kit colors: vibrant royal/cerulean blue for the primary home kit and intense athletic red for the away kit and decisive combat accents.

- **Primary (`#0077B6`) — Home Kit Royal Blue**: Embodies club identity across primary action buttons, key metrics, active tab indicators, and verified club status marks.
- **Secondary (`#D62828`) — Away Kit Combat Red**: Represents athletic friction, critical live-match score indicators, time-out alerts, yellow/red sanction counters, and high-urgency callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#023E8A`) — Deep Valencian Navy**: Grounds headlines, primary navigation bars, and competitive bracket headers with federation-grade authority.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`) — Slate Graphite**: The bedrock for crisp, high-contrast typography, high-density roster data, and structural line work.

### Color Modes
- **Light Mode (Default)**: Bright athletic baseline. Backdrops utilize `#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF` cards framed with fine `#E2E8F0` borders, producing a modern pavilion feel.
- **Dark Mode (Dual Support)**: Nocturnal stadium experience. Deep graphite and navy surfaces (`#0B192C` base, `#132238` container) illuminate the same kit blue (`#0077B6` / `#38BDF8`) and vibrant red (`#D62828`) as high-visibility luminous accents.

## Typography
The typography pairing establishes clear separation between athletic branding and dense administrative data:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers punchy, modern athletic geometry. Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) provide court presence on scoreboards, banner headlines, and match result feeds.
- **Body & Data Tables (Inter)**: Delivers neutral, highly legible performance for rosters, match minute reports, referee notations, and league standings.
- **Tabular Figures**: All numeric variables, match clocks, player squad numbers, and scoreboard figures must declare `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure stability during live updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column grid on desktop screens, an 8-column grid on tablet viewports, and a condensed 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px – 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer canvas margins span `2rem` on desktop and contract to `1rem` on mobile viewports to maximize visual width for game stats and player grids.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing intervals scale along an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`), ensuring match chronologies, schedule brackets, and federation forms remain structured across device types.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through crisp, low-contrast outlines and clean layered surfaces rather than muddy drop shadows, reflecting athletic precision:

- **Surface 0 (Background)**: Clean `#F8FAFC` in light mode (deep nocturnal `#0B192C` in dark mode).
- **Surface 1 (Cards, Modules, Roster Panels)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` anchored by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` (dark mode: `#132238` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border).
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Flyouts, Score Overlays, Modals)**: Elevated `#FFFFFF` with a refined ambient shadow (`0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) and an optional 2px top stroke in primary blue `#0077B6`.
- **Match-Day Focus Elevation**: Live-match score cards and breaking alerts incorporate a left-edge accent stroke (4px solid `#D62828` or `#0077B6`) to signal ongoing action instantly.

## Shapes
The shape language implements a disciplined structural radius (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline):

- **Cards & Modules**: 8px (`0.5rem`) for disciplined containment of tables and news cards.
- **Modals & Match Centers**: 16px (`1rem`) to provide softer separation from the canvas.
- **Pills & Status Tags**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to distinguish temporal match states (periods, timeouts, positions) from functional grid cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button**: Solid `#0077B6` fill with `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), 44px height, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Transitions to hover shade `#023E8A`.
- **Secondary / Action Button**: Solid away red `#D62828` fill with `#FFFFFF` text for live game triggers, registrations, and high-priority operations.
- **Outline / Ghost Button**: White or transparent background with 1.5px solid `#0077B6` border and `#0077B6` text. In dark mode, utilizes light-blue outline.
- **Destructive / Sanction Button**: Soft `#D62828` background at 10% opacity with solid `#D62828` border and text for disciplinary reports and red card tracking.

### Match Scorecards & Score Displays
- Light container (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and subtle left accent stripe (4px kit blue).
- Header row features a time badge pill (`bg-red-50`, text `#D62828`, `label-md`) indicating current minute or final status.
- Center score column uses `score-display` styling in `#0F172A` with tabular figures.
- Opponent and club logos display inside 44px circular or rounded shields flanked by `headline-sm` labels.

### Chips & Player Badges
- **Court Position Pills**: 24px height, `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Fill `#0077B6` at 10% opacity with `#0077B6` text for positions (Extremo, Central, Lateral, Pivote, Portero).
- **Match Status Chips**:
  - *En Juego (Live)*: `#D62828` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
  - *Convocado*: Light slate tint with `#0F172A` text.
  - *Exclusión 2 min*: Amber background with dark text.

### Form Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields**: 44px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#64748B`. Focused state applies a 2px ring in primary kit blue `#0077B6`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square/round with 1.5px border. Checked state fills with `#0077B6` with an interior white checkmark.

### Administrative Tables & Federation Rosters
- Alternating row styling using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Column headers styled with uppercase `label-md` in `#64748B` with a 1px solid bottom border.
- Compact cell height: 40px for statistics; 48px for editable roster rows.
